Jarvis Dashboard

01 / Context

Behind the
project.

Jarvis is an interactive daily-briefing prototype. It brings tasks, schedule and mail into a coordinated dashboard intended to make information easier to scan.

The prototype places different information sources within one visual language, making the experience itself the central artifact of this study.

02 / Challenge

The question.

How might a daily briefing surface important information quickly without overwhelming the user?

03 / Objectives

What mattered.

  • Prioritize information for at-a-glance reading.
  • Bring several everyday workflows into one coherent layout.
  • Explore clear hierarchy and interaction patterns.

04 / Process

From idea to direction.

The interactive prototype includes a daily-status presentation and coordinated panels for tasks, schedule and mail, with hierarchy shaped around the daily briefing.

05 / Solution

What took shape.

An interactive dashboard prototype organized around a personal daily briefing.

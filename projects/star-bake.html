01 / Context

Behind the
project.

Star Bake is a mobile application interface exploration for a bakery and rewards experience. The screen set covers key moments in the customer journey, from account creation to choosing products and keeping track of rewards.

This case study is a place to document the problem, the choices behind the interface and what changed throughout the design process.

02 / Challenge

The question.

How can a bakery app make products easy to discover while keeping account and loyalty experiences straightforward?

03 / Objectives

What mattered.

  • Establish a clear menu and product discovery journey.
  • Make account entry, sign-up and password recovery feel consistent.
  • Give rewards and profile screens a cohesive visual language.

04 / Process

From idea to direction.

05 / Solution

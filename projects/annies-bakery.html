Case Study / 08

Annie's Bakery

A friendly bakery identity grounded in a recognizable visual personality.

DisciplineBrand identity · Rebranding
FocusHeritage · Packaging · Identity
ExploreHeritage · Renewal · Identity
01 / Annie's Bakery · The logo

01 / Context

Behind the
project.

Annie's Bakery has been part of its community for more than 30 years. When the owners' eldest daughter decided to take the reins of the family business, she wanted to give the bakery a fresh visual identity without losing the history that made it special.

We worked to create a minimalist, contemporary identity connected to the bakery's everyday craft. Because a stand mixer plays a central role in preparing many of its products, we brought that familiar tool into the logo. We also incorporated silhouettes of bread and croissants — motifs the new owner has tattooed on her own body — into the brand's supporting graphics and packaging direction.

02 / Challenge

The question.

How could we modernize a bakery with more than three decades of history while honoring its family legacy, daily craft and the new owner's personal connection to baking?

03 / Objectives

What mattered.

  • Honor more than 30 years of family bakery tradition while welcoming a new generation of leadership.
  • Create a clean, minimalist and contemporary identity.
  • Reference the stand mixer used in the bakery's daily preparations through the logo.
  • Adapt the owner's meaningful bread and croissant silhouettes into supporting graphics for packaging.

04 / Process

From idea to direction.

The process began by understanding the bakery's history and the eldest daughter's ambition for its next chapter. We identified the stand mixer as a recognizable element of the business's everyday work and integrated it into the identity concept. To give the broader system a personal connection to its new owner, we drew from her bread and croissant tattoos as inspiration for minimalist illustrations that could extend onto packaging.

05 / Solution

What took shape.

We developed a contemporary visual identity built around the stand mixer, supported by simplified bread and croissant silhouettes that link the design to the owner's personal story. The logo and brand sheet provide a consistent foundation for the bakery's evolving image and packaging direction.

THE STORY BEHIND THE REBRAND

A family legacy.
A fresh chapter.

Rather than replacing the bakery's personality, we looked for meaningful details that could carry its history into a more contemporary visual identity.

01 / HERITAGE

Over 30 years of baking.

02 / NEW LEADERSHIP

The eldest daughter's vision.

As she stepped forward to lead the bakery, she saw an opportunity to renew its image and shape its next chapter.

03 / THE LOGO

A mixer at the heart of the craft.

The stand mixer, used to prepare many of the bakery's products, became the central reference for a minimalist, contemporary logo.

04 / PERSONAL DETAILS

Bread, croissants and packaging.

Silhouettes inspired by the new owner's own bread and croissant tattoos connect the brand's packaging illustrations to her personal story.

06 / Visual explorations

In the details.

The logo leads the story, followed by the identity sheet. The bread and croissant motifs are part of the packaging design direction.

07 / Results

What was
created.

The rebrand brought together a logo concept inspired by a stand mixer, a minimalist contemporary identity and a graphic direction using bread and croissant motifs for packaging. The work connects the next generation of leadership to the bakery's established heritage.

Case Study / 09

Las Marías Market

An immersive market experience celebrating independent makers, women-led stories and the adventure of building a business.

DisciplineBrand identity · Marketplace
FocusBrand storytelling · Web
ExploreEntrepreneurship · Identity · Discovery
01 / Las Marías Market · The logo

01 / Context

Behind the
project.

Las Marías Market began as a way for entrepreneurs and small businesses to bring their products to market. Rather than following the scale of a traditional retail store, it creates room for carefully curated objects, handmade goods and products made in smaller batches. Today, the business distributes products from more than 50 independent brands.

Many of the brands it represents are led or represented by women. We wanted that reality to be central to the identity: a marketplace where every product carries a story of someone starting, building or growing a business. The concept invites visitors into an immersive journey of discovery — an adventure through the beginnings of entrepreneurship.

02 / Challenge

The question.

How could we create a recognizable identity for a marketplace of more than 50 independent brands while celebrating the women behind many of those businesses and making the discovery of each product feel like an adventure?

03 / Objectives

What mattered.

  • Represent the diversity of handmade, small-batch and thoughtfully curated products.
  • Celebrate the independent businesses and the women who represent many of the brands.
  • Build the logo around a coin and a female figure as the marketplace's central symbols.
  • Translate that identity into a discovery-led, immersive website concept.

04 / Process

From idea to direction.

We started with the story behind the market: entrepreneurs finding a way to sell what they make and curate, often before reaching the scale of conventional retail. The coin emerged as the central image of commerce and opportunity. A woman's figure became part of that symbol to reflect the women who represent the majority of the brands. We then extended this narrative into the digital direction, imagining a shopping experience that feels like discovering the stories and beginnings behind each business.

05 / Solution

What took shape.

The Las Marías Market identity centers on a coin featuring a female figure — a symbol of commerce and the women behind many of the market's independent brands. The accompanying website concept presents the market as more than a place to purchase objects: it is an invitation to discover handmade products, curated finds and the entrepreneurial journeys behind them.

THE STORY BEHIND THE MARKET

Small beginnings.
Big stories.

Four ideas shape the identity: a home for independent makers, the meaning of the coin, the women behind the businesses and the adventure of discovery.

01 / THE PURPOSE

A market for independent makers.

Las Marías Market gives entrepreneurs, makers and small-scale businesses a way to share handmade goods, limited-run creations and thoughtfully curated finds.

02 / THE COIN

Commerce with a human story.

A coin sits at the center of the logo, representing trade, opportunity and the earliest steps of building a business.

03 / THE WOMEN

The people behind the brands.

A female figure emerges from the coin, acknowledging that most of the independent brands represented by the market are connected to women.

04 / THE EXPERIENCE

Discovery as an adventure.

The website direction imagines a shopping journey that invites people to explore each business, its products and the story of how it began.

06 / Visual explorations

In the details.

The logo takes center stage. The website concept follows, translating the idea of an immersive marketplace into a digital experience.

07 / Motion and campaigns

The market in motion.

Short-form campaigns and content showing how the market connects independent makers with its community.

08 / Results

What was
created.

We developed an identity concept that connects commerce with the women behind many of Las Marías Market's independent brands. The central coin symbol and its female figure carry that story into a logo, supported by a digital marketplace design that frames shopping as discovery.

Star Bake

A bakery and loyalty app. These are the original screens, rebuilt in code so they can be tapped through: sign in, browse the menu, open a product, check the points.

  1. The brand seal and one product photo carry the screen. One button to sign in, with sign-up as a text link below it.

  2. A large greeting sets the tone before the form. One field accepts a username, an email, or a phone number, so nobody has to remember which they used.

  3. The same layout on white, which separates creating an account from returning to one. Password rules sit under the fields they apply to.

  4. One field and one button. The headline says it in the customer's words, and the seal repeats as a pattern to keep the screen in the brand.

  5. Search, the weekly deal, then three categories. Every product can be added to the bag or saved from the grid without opening it. Tap the baguette to see its page.

  6. Photo, name, weight, and price first; nutrition facts below. Quantity is set next to the price it changes.

  7. The points balance and the code to scan at the counter sit side by side. Rewards are grouped by what they cost in points.

Interactive rebuild · sample data · logo and UI unchanged

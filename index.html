ABOUT

A LITTLE
About me

My résumé says marketing. But the part I always loved was never the campaign — it was building the brand, the message, the identity, the thing itself. That instinct for building is the actual thread running through my career.

Marketing and branding taught me how businesses, customers, and identity work. Design, code, and AI gave me new ways to turn that understanding into real products and experiences — which is the work I want to keep doing.

MY APPROACH

Think. Design.
BUILD.

Strategy and execution are not separate disciplines in my work. They inform each other.

01 / THINK

Find the right problem.

Product strategy, research, user journeys and brand thinking. I connect business goals to human needs before moving pixels.

02 / DESIGN

Make it make sense.

UX/UI, identity systems, prototypes and interaction design. I craft useful experiences with a distinctive point of view.

03 / BUILD

Bring it into the world.

Front-end implementation, AI-assisted workflows, testing and iteration. I move from concept to working interface myself.
